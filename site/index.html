TRUST IS A DESIGN CONSTRAINT

Useful control.
Honest limits.

Designed for the owner’s devices or devices they are explicitly authorized to manage. Not a way around operating-system consent.

A / ACCESS

Rights are explicit

The protected owner is defined by configuration. Other accounts can be users, guests, or blocked; user access is scoped by device and action grants.

MQTT uses TLS and HMAC-signed messages. Payload encryption depends on ENCRYPT_PAYLOAD and is not assumed active in every local setup.

B / REALITY

Status is not certainty

An online indicator means a recent heartbeat, not guaranteed availability. IP-based location is approximate, not GPS.

Camera, microphone, screen capture, and other protected capabilities need an authorized command and applicable OS permission. Sending a command alone proves nothing about physical completion.
